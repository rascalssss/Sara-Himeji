What it does

Commands

/anime name: OnimaiRating, format, episodes and a cover for any series.AniList
/manga · /lnSame lookup for manga and light novels, with a volume picker.AniList
/season season: Fall year: 2026Most popular shows of any season, paged.AniList
/nextCountdown to the next episode of a series.AniList
/follow anime: OnimaiOnly post episode alerts for the shows your server follows.Manage Server
/anilist user: TechnoAnime and manga stats, days watched, favourites.AniList
/anibirthdaysCharacters with a birthday today.AniList
/traceUpload a screenshot, get the episode and timestamp.trace.moe
/character · /studio · /genre · /trailerBios, studio rankings, top 10 by genre, trailers.AniList
/setchannelChoose where news, chapters and episode alerts go, and which role is pinged.Administrator

Setup

Three steps

1

Add Mahiro to your server with the button above.

2

Run /setchannel to pick feed channels and an optional ping role.

3

Optional: /follow your shows so alerts stay quiet.

Where the news comes from

Real feeds

Anime News Network / MyAnimeList / Crunchyroll News / AnimeSchedule / MangaUpdates / AniList / trace.moe
