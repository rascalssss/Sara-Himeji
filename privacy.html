Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 October 2026

1. Controller

The person in the legal notice. Contact: your-email@example.com

2. What the bot stores

  • Server ID, the channel IDs and role IDs you pick with /setchannel, and the anime list from /follow.
  • IDs of articles, episodes and chapters already posted, to avoid duplicates.
  • Operator settings such as an ad whitelist.

The bot does not store message content, direct messages, or profiles of individual members. Technical error logs on the host can contain command names and error text.

3. Operator log

When the bot joins or leaves a server, the server name, ID, member count, and the owner's Discord name and ID are posted to a private log channel for the operator.

4. Data sent to other services

  • Discord delivers all messages and commands. Its privacy policy applies.
  • AniList receives the search text or username you enter in lookup commands.
  • trace.moe receives the image link you give /trace.
  • Amazon receives you only if you click a buy button; Amazon's own policy and cookies then apply.

News feeds are read without sending any user data. Some of these services are outside the EU/EEA and process data under their own terms.

5. Legal basis and retention

Running the bot you asked for is a legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Server data is deleted automatically when the bot is removed from your server. You can also ask for deletion by email. Posted-ID records are cleared periodically or on request.

6. This website

The site sets no cookies, uses no analytics, and loads no external fonts or scripts. The hosting provider (host name) records IP address and request time in server logs for security and operation (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). If the host adds its own cookies or scripts, those are outside the operator's control.

7. Your rights

You can request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability and object to processing (Art. 15-21 GDPR) by email. You may complain to a data protection authority; for Hesse this is the Hessischer Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, Wiesbaden.

8. Children

The bot is meant for people old enough to use Discord and collects no age data. A parent or guardian can write to request deletion of a server's data.
